LBP SALES INTELLIGENCE TRACKER - PROJECT DOCUMENTATION

PROJECT OBJECTIVE The workbook is designed as an Enterprise Sales
Intelligence Tracker for Love Beauty & Planet (LBP). It separates Raw
Data, Helper Logic, Pivot Layer, Analysis, and Dashboard so it can be
refreshed every month by appending new data.

================================================== WORKBOOK STRUCTURE
==================================================

00_README - Workbook documentation - Update instructions - Business
definitions - Workbook architecture

Questions answered: - What is this workbook? - How do I update it? -
What does each sheet do?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

01_Offtakes_Base_File Purpose: - Historical raw data repository. -
Source of truth. - New months are appended, never deleted.

Rules: - No manual edits. - Excel Table: tbl_Offtakes.

Main fields: Monthly Period, Month, Quarter, Year, Customer, Category,
Ingredient, Segment, Product Title, Basepack, Offtakes Value (Lakhs).

------------------------------------------------------------------------

02_Helper

Acts as the engine of the workbook.

Sections: A Workbook Settings B Calendar Table C Master Lists D Time
Dimensions E Product Titles F KPI Engine G Alert Engine H Dashboard
Engine I Validation Lists

Dynamic lists: Customer Category Ingredient Segment Basepack Product
Title Quarter Month Year

Purpose: - Dropdowns - Validation - Future slicers - Reusable
calculations

------------------------------------------------------------------------

03A_PivotCache

Backend only.

Contains Pivot Tables: - Monthly Sales - Customer Sales - Category
Contribution - Ingredient Contribution - Segment Contribution - Product
Title - Basepack

No charts. No formatting. Feeds every analysis page.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

03_Business_Overview

Purpose: Answer: ‘How is the business performing overall?’

Contains: - KPI Cards - Business Highlights - Monthly Trend - Customer
Contribution - Category Contribution - Ingredient Contribution - Segment
Contribution

KPI Cards: - Total Sales - Total Customers - Total Basepacks - Total
Products - Total Ingredients - Total Categories

Business Highlights: - Highest Sales Month - Lowest Sales Month - Top
Customer - Top Category - Top Ingredient - Top Segment

Charts: Monthly Trend (Line) Customer Contribution (Bar) Category
Contribution (Donut) Ingredient Contribution (Horizontal Bar) Segment
Contribution (Horizontal Bar)

Questions answered: - How much have we sold? - Which customer
contributes most? - Which category dominates? - Which ingredient is
strongest? - Which segment leads?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

04_Top_Performers

Purpose: Identify the highest-performing business drivers.

Summary Cards: - Top Customer - Top Product - Top Basepack - Top
Ingredient - Top Category - Top Segment

Each card displays: - Name - Sales - Contribution %

Charts: Top Customers Top Products Top Basepacks Top Ingredients Top
Categories Top Segments

Questions answered: - Who is No.1? - Which products generate most
revenue? - Which customer drives business? - Which ingredient performs
best?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

05_Opportunity_Areas

Purpose: Identify where management should take action.

Summary Cards: - Lowest Customer - Lowest Product - Lowest Basepack -
Lowest Ingredient - Lowest Category - Lowest Segment

Charts: Bottom 10 analysis.

Also includes: - Risk Indicator - Recommended Actions - Opportunity
observations

Questions answered: - Which customers are underperforming? - Which
products need promotion? - Which categories require attention?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

06_Drill_Down (Designed)

Purpose: Explain WHY performance looks the way it does.

Cross Analysis: Customer x Category Customer x Ingredient Customer x
Segment Ingredient x Segment Category x Ingredient Category x Segment

Questions answered: - Which customer buys which category? - Which
ingredient drives which segment? - Which category depends on one
customer? - Which combinations create the most sales?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

OVERALL DATA FLOW

01_Offtakes_Base_File ↓ 02_Helper ↓ 03A_PivotCache ↓
03_Business_Overview 04_Top_Performers 05_Opportunity_Areas
06_Drill_Down ↓ 07_Business_Alerts (Planned) 08_KPI_Scorecard (Planned)
09_Executive_Dashboard (Planned)

================================================== ENTERPRISE PRINCIPLES
==================================================

-   Historical data is never deleted.
-   New months are appended.
-   Values displayed in Lakhs.
-   PivotTables stay in PivotCache.
-   Dashboard sheets contain charts and insights only.
-   Helper sheet powers the workbook.
-   Each page answers a specific business theme.
-   Shared slicers control multiple PivotTables.
